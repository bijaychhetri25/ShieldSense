# Data Card — ShieldSense Synthetic Scam-Message Dataset v0.2

**Author:** Jaskaran Singh — Data & Labelling Lead
**Date:** 24 September 2026
**Status:** Draft

## 1. Dataset Name

ShieldSense Synthetic Scam-Message Dataset v0.2

## 2. Purpose

Supports training and evaluation of a lightweight text classifier that labels SMS/chat messages as Low, Medium or High risk. Used to compare a rules-based baseline against a trained classifier.

## 3. Source

- Synthetic messages: Generated by the project team using patterns from public scam-awareness resources.
- Public datasets: None used at this stage. If used, licensing will be documented here.
- No real personal messages: No real SMS, no real phone numbers, no real names, no scraped inboxes.

## 4. Size

- Target: 150–250 messages
- Current: 200 messages
- Distribution: Balanced across Low, Medium, High

## 5. Labelling

- Taxonomy: ShieldSense Risk Taxonomy v2.0
- Protocol: ShieldSense Labelling Protocol v1.0
- Labellers: All four team members
- Inter-rater agreement: Cohen's kappa (target ≥ 0.61)
- Adjudication: Documented in the agreement report

## 6. Limitations

- Synthetic data may not capture the full range of real scam messages.
- English-language short-text messages only.
- No URLs are resolved live.
- Hard negatives are included but may not cover every legitimate service pattern.
- Bias may exist in the synthetic generation process.

## 7. Ethical Constraints

- Synthetic and publicly available data only.
- No real personal messages, ever.
- No real phone numbers or names.
- English short-text only.
- No public deployment.
